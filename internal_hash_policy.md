# 🔐 Internal Hash Policy

## 🧬 Why Hashes Matter

Sample hashes included in this project reflect real binaries produced in a controlled QEMU environment. Each represents a unique builder output version.

## ❗ Why We Don’t Share the Samples

- Legal risk: Distributing functional RAT payloads, even for research, may violate malware laws.
- Ethical concerns: Sharing may result in misuse or accidental deployment.
- We are committed to defensive security, not propagation.

## 📂 What We Do Share

- SHA256 hash of each sample
- Static metadata (entropy, imports, capabilities)
- YARA rules, CAPA output, DIE output

All content is derived, not executable.

## 🚫 What We Do Not Share

- No PE binaries
- No decrypted payloads
- No direct links to builders

If you need deeper access for research purposes, contact us with institutional credentials — but we reserve the right to decline.
